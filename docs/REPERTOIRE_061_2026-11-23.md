# 0.6.1 — recurring phrases and source repertoire

Historical 0.6.1 report. Basic Music measures 13–14 and ornament playback are superseded by the [0.7 review](EXPRESSION_AB_070_2026-11-23.md); the other repertoire source checks remain applicable.

The 0.6 reference-v2 Basic Music edition still contained adjacent-pitch artifacts around measures 13 and 27, and missed ties in the recurring phrase in 28. This correction uses the supplied score screenshots and checks the recording's attacks rather than rerunning an audio model and claiming that as a faithful score.

- Measure 12's high sixteenth-note run and mid-bar return to the ordinary treble clef are retained. The effective clef is explicitly restated at subsequent bar starts to avoid renderer carry-over after a mid-bar change.
- The upper phrase beginning in 13 is C4–F4–G4–F4–D4 / G4–C5–D5–C5–B4. The same melody from the second half of 27 through the first half of 28 is checked with identical pitch/onset/held-duration expectations. Adjacent lower extras in those upper-staff events are removed; this does not snap chromatic B naturals into the key signature.
- G4 and B4 in the recurring phrase are held across the two written sixteenths, not struck again. The lower F3 tie is also restored. Lower attacks in 13/27 are separated rather than duplicated as adjacent F3/G3 pairs; the D3 bass is retained. Legitimate lower-staff differences between occurrences remain.
- The upper rests and five short notes in 14 and the end of 28 keep their musical positions. They are not filled with invented accompaniment or shortened using a noise filter.
- The reference writer emits the reviewed source events and pitch-specific ties directly. It no longer sends this reference score through the generic audio writer's voice partitioning. Full downloadable MIDI and app MusicXML have equal note events, including the complete repeat: 618 events per traversal / 1,236 played events. A lower Bb3 tie in measure 15 had a missing start marker; its start is now explicitly retained too.

Old v1/v2 library records are preserved. Reference v3 has its own ID, so users can open the correction without losing edits. The audio inference model remains Spotify Basic Pitch; this is a reference-notation correction, not a newly trained audio model.

Independent same-pitch voices also exposed an export defect: putting overlapping equal pitches on one MIDI channel changes which note-off belongs to which attack. MIDI export now partitions only those overlaps into separate channels, preserving their independent releases; normal polyphony stays together. The standard limit of 15 melodic MIDI channels is checked explicitly instead of silently reusing a channel. Percussion keeps channel 10.

Optional maintainer rebuild: `python3 scripts/build-classical-demos.py` (uv / pinned python-ly 0.9.9). Ordinary `npm run build` needs no Python/LilyPond and verifies the retained sources against their SHA256 lock.

The source-MIDI comparison establishes the principal written events, not expressive performance accuracy. Mozart's PDF contains the complete movement, while the playable excerpt is explicitly only measures 1–12.
